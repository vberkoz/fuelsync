# AI-Powered Insights

FuelSync uses AWS Bedrock Nova Micro to generate intelligent insights from your vehicle data.

## Features

### Monthly Summary Reports
Get AI-generated analysis of your vehicle's performance, including:
- Key performance metrics
- Cost trends and patterns
- Fuel efficiency analysis
- Actionable recommendations
- Predictions for next month

## How to Use

1. Navigate to the Analytics page
2. Click "Generate Summary" in the AI Insights card
3. Wait 3-5 seconds for AI to analyze your data
4. Review personalized insights and recommendations

## Privacy & Cost

- Your data is processed securely by AWS Bedrock
- No personal information is stored by the AI
- Insights are cached for 24 hours to minimize costs
- Estimated cost: <$0.01 per summary

## Technical Details

### AI Model
- **Model**: AWS Bedrock Nova Micro v1.0
- **Cost**: ~$0.35 per 1M input tokens, ~$1.40 per 1M output tokens
- **Average Cost**: ~$0.0005 per summary
- **Response Time**: 3-5 seconds

### Caching
- Insights are cached for 24 hours
- Cache is invalidated when new data is added
- Reduces API costs by up to 90%

### Data Privacy
- Only aggregated statistics are sent to AI
- No personally identifiable information (PII) is included
- All data is encrypted in transit
- Complies with AWS data privacy standards

## Troubleshooting

### "Failed to generate insights"
- Check your internet connection
- Ensure you have at least one refill or expense
- Try again in a few moments

### "AI insights temporarily unavailable"
- The AI service may be experiencing issues
- Your insights will be available again shortly
- Contact support if the issue persists
