# Analytics Enhancement Ideas

Based on the current Analytics page (`packages/app/src/pages/Analytics.tsx`), here are additional helpful analytics that could be displayed using existing refills/expenses data.

## Current Analytics
- Fuel stats (total cost, refills count, volume, avg price/unit, avg refill cost)
- Expenses stats (total cost, count, avg expense)
- Efficiency metrics (current, average, full-tank, city/highway breakdown)
- Total costs
- Charts: fuel consumption, costs over time, efficiency trends

## Additional Analytics Ideas

### 1. Cost Per Distance Analytics
- Cost per mile/km (fuel + maintenance combined)
- Monthly cost per distance trends
- Compare cost efficiency between vehicles

### 2. Expense Category Breakdown
- Pie chart showing expense distribution by category (maintenance, repairs, insurance, etc.)
- Top 3 most expensive categories
- Category spending trends over time

### 3. Refill Patterns & Insights
- Average days between refills
- Most frequent refill locations/stations
- Refill frequency trends (seasonal patterns)
- Partial vs full tank ratio

### 4. Distance & Usage Analytics
- Total distance driven
- Average monthly mileage
- Distance per refill (range analysis)
- Usage intensity (daily/weekly driving patterns)

### 5. Price Intelligence
- Fuel price trends over time
- Best/worst fuel prices paid
- Price volatility indicators
- Savings from finding cheaper stations

### 6. Maintenance Insights
- Time since last maintenance by category
- Upcoming maintenance predictions based on mileage
- Maintenance cost per mile/km
- Service interval tracking

### 7. Comparative Analytics (for multi-vehicle users)
- Vehicle cost comparison
- Efficiency comparison between vehicles
- Usage patterns comparison

### 8. Financial Projections
- Monthly/yearly cost projections based on current usage
- Budget vs actual spending
- Cost per month trends

## Priority Recommendations

The most valuable additions would be:
1. **Expense category breakdown** - Shows where money is being spent
2. **Cost per distance** - Key metric for vehicle cost efficiency
3. **Refill patterns** - Helps optimize refueling behavior

These provide actionable insights for users to optimize their vehicle expenses and usage patterns.
